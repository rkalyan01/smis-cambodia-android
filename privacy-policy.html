SMIS Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sanitation Management Information System (SMIS) mobile application and website collect, use, and protect information. SMIS is operated by Innovative Solution Pvt. Ltd. and is related to the SMIS web platform at https://smis.muninfosys.com/ .

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as name, email, and login credentials.
  • Operational data you enter for sanitation services and reporting.
  • Photos you capture or upload for service documentation.
  • Location data to support map and service tracking features.
  • Device and usage data such as app version and error logs.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve SMIS features and services.
  • Enable workflows such as mapping, reporting, and task verification.
  • Support user accounts, authentication, and access control.
  • Maintain app reliability, security, and customer support.

Permissions

  • Camera: used to capture photos for service documentation.
  • Location: used to display and track service locations on maps.
  • Storage/Media: used to attach and store images and files.

You can manage these permissions in your device settings. If you deny a permission, some features may not work as intended.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with authorized partners and service providers that support SMIS operations, or when required by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we strive to protect your data.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide SMIS services and to comply with legal and operational requirements.

Children's Privacy

SMIS is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post changes on this page and update the effective date.
